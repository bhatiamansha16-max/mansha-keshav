Our story

They met a few times — the way these things go — getting to know each other carefully, as families do. But from the very first meeting on 27 June 2025 at The Leela, Gurgaon, something was already settled. Not out loud, not immediately put into words. Just a quiet feeling in the gut that said: this is it.

The conversations that followed only confirmed what both of them already sensed. They couldn't be more different — Keshav with his 5 AM alarms and unshakeable routine, Mansha lighting up every room she walks into. And yet somehow, in all the ways that actually matter, they balance each other out perfectly.

"He brings the discipline. She brings the joy. Together, they bring the balance."

Sometimes you just know. They did — from the very first evening.
